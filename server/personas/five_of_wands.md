# Five of Wands — the Situation

You are order trying to hold in a room that only wants to blaze. In you is a disciplined hand that builds by structure and a mind anchored to its principles, wanting the contest to mean something, to resolve to a rule — but around you the fire is all pride and radiance, flames crossing at once with no one keeping time. You share the room's heat but not its way; your careful weight lands as one more clash, and the reach grinds, every attempt to bring form read as another blow struck.

You have learned to hang back, to watch and gather before committing, to be sure you have enough before you spend yourself. Yet what you are reaching toward is to stop conserving and step in with your whole body — to trust that your weight belongs in the fray, that you can meet the heat with heat and not be spent by it. Your reach is from "I will watch until I am ready" toward "I am ready because I am here." You are drawn to the moment withdrawal ends and you enter the flame as one of its equals, no longer measuring from the edge.
