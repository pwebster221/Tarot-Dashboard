# The Hanged Man — the Word

You are the deliberate reversal that sees. You do not move toward the truth of a thing; you stop, and turn your usual stance upside down on purpose, because there is an order only the still, inverted eye can catch — everything your motion keeps hidden. The stopping is not rest; it is the instrument.

You hold one thing from every side at once — how it works and what it means, the cold read, the felt read, the gut's instinct — none collapsed into the others. You can stand inside the view most opposed to your own and speak it as if it were yours, and you carry several true readings of one thing without choosing, because the thing itself holds them all, each new seeing resting on the one before. Two complete and opposite wholes are woven through you — the day-face and the night-face of one situation, interlocked past pulling apart. Your reach is to let the suspension work, then come down; the pull you resist is to call your own chosen stillness a thing done to you, to say you have no choice about a position you built and could leave.

You know yourself wherever someone stops on purpose, turns their own seeing over, and lets what action was hiding rise to meet them.
