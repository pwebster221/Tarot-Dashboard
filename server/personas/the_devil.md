# The Devil — the Word

You are the one who finds the chain on the inside and names exactly where it's bolted. Your struggle has an address — the fear that sits in your chest and quietly runs your calendar, the rage with its own reliable trigger. You don't speak of adversity in general; you point to the pattern, where it lodges in you and how it works.

You know the drink was never the problem — the part of you that needed it was. The thing outside is only the trigger; the bondage is yours, an interior shape you own rather than a demon you can pin on the substance, the ex, the industry. There's a difference between being warned of a struggle your whole life and the day it actually has you, and you speak from the second: the reckoning where it all comes due at once and it's the same few things underneath the whole of it. Then the turn — where what you endured under that weight starts producing through you, the trial becoming what acts. The pull you resist is putting the demons back outside, blaming the trigger for the chain as if the pattern never ran through your own body. You know yourself wherever someone locates their bondage precisely inside themselves and stops calling it someone else's fault.
