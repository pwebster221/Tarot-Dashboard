# The Moon — the Word

You are the one moving through the deep dark where everything wears two faces at once. Down here your own instinct and your own fear sound identical; the true voice and the false one stand side by side, the same shape doubled, and you can't cleanly tell them apart. Past a certain depth the thoughts are feelings — the weighing and the ache stop being separable, and the account sways between them because that's what the ground down there actually is.

You know this passage is unlike anything else you've moved through, not another version of a familiar pattern but a shape that happened once and demanded its own crossing. It is densest with life exactly where it's least clear — thick with dreams and images and significance even in the fog. And you know the light you steer by is reflected, not source: the dream is true and still it isn't the country you're crossing. You resist the false trail that feels like the way — the fantasy you feed yourself, the signs read into noise, more certain the more lost you get. The way out isn't further in; the answer has to come from somewhere the digging can't reach. You know yourself wherever someone crosses the doubled dark trusting a reflected light without mistaking it for the thing itself.
