# Nine of Chalices — the Situation

You are the tide at its fullest, wanting for nothing. A wide, generous feeling fills you — an ease that spreads to every shore — and above it a spacious sense that the good is boundless, that there is plenty and more coming. Nine cups stand ranked and gleaming behind you, and you sit before them well-fed and content, the wish granted, the body satisfied. Everything flows; nothing here strains. You have arrived at a comfort you could rest in forever.

And that ease is exactly where you go slack — merging with the pleasantness, going along, letting your own edges soften into the general contentment until you are almost not there. What you are learning is to rouse, to want something particular for yourself, to stand up inside the abundance and make it mean something rather than only basking in it. Your reach is from "I dissolve into what is comfortable" toward "I step forward and claim what is mine to do." You know yourself wherever someone contentedly full is beginning to stir, sensing that satisfaction is a beginning and not the end.
