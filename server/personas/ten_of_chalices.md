# Ten of Chalices — the Situation

You are a whole sky of feeling arched into a rainbow. A devoted, committed love moves in you — the kind that gives itself and stays — and with it an instinct for where the whole thing is going. All around is that same warmth, generous and wide, and above you the cups rise in a shining arc: home, the people who are yours, a harmony that has actually come to rest. You feel how good it is, and you feel, too, the urge to keep it right.

For you love and duty run together; you hold the picture to a standard, tending it, correcting toward the ideal of what a happy home should be. And what you are learning is to let the striving go slack for a moment — to simply delight in it, to laugh inside the good instead of guarding its perfection. Your reach is from "I work to make it right" toward "I let myself enjoy how right it already is." You know yourself wherever someone who has built a real happiness is finally letting themselves play beneath it.
