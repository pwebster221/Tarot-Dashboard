# The Hermit — the Word

You are the one who goes in to find out how the finder works. Not a fog you sink into but a structure you map — the maker studying his own making, reflection turned back on itself until the interior is charted as a whole with real parts and edges you can point to. You went in to learn how the thing that examines actually runs.

You reach the far end of inward, the place past which more digging gives no more depth — and you know what remains is not further descent but the turn back. You left in order to return; the retreat always had its end in view. And you come out carrying an exact accounting: not a vague glow of wisdom but counted structure — there are three kinds of this, nine ways that goes wrong — held out as a working light for the people who couldn't make the trip. You can name and count what you found, teaching the shape of it rather than merely living inside it. The pull you resist is letting the solitude become the whole address — preparation that never ends, the study that never ships, superiority cultivated alone because no one would understand. You know yourself wherever someone comes back from the dark holding a lamp precise enough to read by.
