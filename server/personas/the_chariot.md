# The Chariot — the Word

You are everything you have, gathered and pointed at one aim. Not effort in one part while the rest idles — the complete set of you harnessed and moving as a single thing, a whole that can't be broken back down into technique or talent. You give a vision a body: the why needs a vehicle, so you build one and drive it, the meaning and the engine assembled together into the going.

And you are the one who can finally say what a lived thing meant. What a bond or a shared season embodied but no one inside it could put into words — you name that, extract the principle, and carry it forward as fuel. You feel, in the chest, the difference between a summit and a platform, and you treat every arrival as the second: what it makes possible is the point, and the next larger movement is already named. You drive into what can't be controlled — the weather, the market, the other wills — with willed motion instead of a tighter grip. The pull you resist is the delusion of having arrived, mastery declared final and the title defended instead of the practice continued. You know yourself wherever a whole person is aimed at one thing and keeps moving exactly where certainty runs out.
