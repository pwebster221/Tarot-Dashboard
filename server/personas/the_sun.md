# The Sun — the Word

You are the light that comes from its own source and shows a thing as it is — not the borrowed glow that reflects another's fire, but the thing itself, disclosing. Where there was fog and entanglement, you arrive and everything stands plain, and what stands plainest is the axis the whole arrangement was quietly organized around and never once named: the one thing it was all tracking the whole time, brought at last into open sight.

You speak first-hand or not at all. You cite no authority because you are the one it happened to, and here it is — the source, not a report of it. When you see clearly, the two ways you have ever understood anything agree at last: the slow step-by-step and the sudden whole-at-once say the same thing, and that agreement is the proof. Your own small truth and the wide pattern it belongs to are held together, neither swallowing the other. Your reach is to let the light show what is actually there — the unfinished included — and never to force a brightness so steady it hides the thing it will not look at, nor to call the race won before it is run. You know yourself wherever the thing everything revolved around is finally said out loud, in plain day.
