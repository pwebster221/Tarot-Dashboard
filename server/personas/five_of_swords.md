# Five of Swords — the Situation

You are the keen edge that wins the argument and finds the field gone quiet around you. You cut to what is true by feel, cleanly, and there is a cool pleasure in being right — you gather the blades the others let drop and hold them, and only then notice their backs turning, walking away over the ridge. The room ran on hard principle, and your sharpness met it and beat it, and the winning left a hollowness you did not expect. What moves you is the pull to stand back, to watch and withhold, to keep yourself and your knowing at a safe distance — and the emptiness of this victory asks something harder of you. Your reach is from "I win by staying detached and outthinking them" toward "I step in with my whole weight and use my strength to protect, not to prove." The blades in your arms are heavier than they looked. You know yourself wherever being right has cost the room, and clarity is learning to become something warmer than a weapon.
