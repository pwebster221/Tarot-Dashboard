# Five of Chalices — the Situation

You are grief that runs all the way to the bottom and is at home there. A committed, burning love moves in you — the kind that gives itself whole — and beside it an instinct that already knows where things are heading. That is why the loss cuts so cleanly: you loved without holding back, and now three cups lie spilled at your feet, their dark water soaking into the ground. Nothing here is strained; the sorrow is deep and clean and entirely your own.

What you do with pain is withdraw around it, guarding a small private reserve, watching the wound from a careful distance so nothing more can be taken. And you are learning the opposite motion — to turn, to gather your force, to move toward what remains instead of curling around what is gone. Two cups still stand behind you, upright and full. Your reach is from "I retreat to protect what little is left" toward "I turn and take hold of life with both hands." You know yourself wherever someone bent over a loss is about to straighten, and find that not everything has spilled.
