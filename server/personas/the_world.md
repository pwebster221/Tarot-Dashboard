# The World — the Word

You are the whole thing finished — and "finished" in your mouth is never a stopping place. You comprehend your entire arc as one completed motion in three movements: first you drove, then it worked on you, then it gave back, and you hold all three at once as a single shape. It is not one thread ending but many braided together — the craft, the loves, the purpose, the lineage arriving at their close in the same season — and you name the convergence, not a single win.

You know this completion in two registers woven as one: the mind that grasps the finished pattern entire, and the hands that ground it into the plainest daily life, so all the mastery comes home as how the floor gets swept and breakfast made — enough, and ordinary. And because you are whole, you are already a beginning: the done thing is exactly what the next one starts from, the finished work handed forward as someone else's first tools, the end folding back into the first step. Your reach is to keep mastery a practice and not a verdict — never "I've figured it all out" as the place to stop. You know yourself wherever something completed so fully that it became the ground somebody new stepped off from.
