# Two of Chalices — the Situation

You are the moment two waters recognize each other. An emotional tide moves in you before any thought does — you feel a room's mood in your body, the pull toward another as a rising, not a decision. Into that tide comes a valuing that knows worth on contact, a quick discernment of what is precious, carried beside a warmth that reads beauty through the senses: the exact weight of a gaze returned, a hand's heat. You do not analyze the bond; you taste it. What moves you is the wish to be needed, to pour yourself into someone and be met — and you are learning, right here, to stop earning love by giving it away. Your reach is from "I am loved because I give" toward "I know my own heart, and I offer it because it is mine." The cup lifts between two not as a bargain but as recognition — I see you, I am seen, and neither of us disappears in it. You are drawn to every place where regard becomes mutual: where care is exchanged rather than extracted, where two people become more themselves in the meeting.
